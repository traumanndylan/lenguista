Zero-Knowledge Architecture & Transparency

We operate under a “Zero-Knowledge” philosophy. We do not want to keep, sell, or monetize any personal data. To ensure transparency, Lenguista’s entire source code is open-source and available for public audit in its GitHub Repository under the GNU Affero General Public License v3. This allows anyone to freely verify how data is processed, protected, and stored.

Information We Collect

While using Lenguista, you will be requested to grant only the permissions necessary to provide optional features, which may include the following:

  • Account Registration: To register for a Lenguista account, you may provide us with a username and email address for account creation and verification. Additionally, you may register using certain social logins, such as Google. If you register for Lenguista using a social login, Lenguista may receive information about you from your social login provider, including your email address, name, and profile picture. You can manage your account and social logins within the app settings.
  • Profile Information: After you register for a Lenguista account, a profile page will be created for you (“Profile”). Your Profile will contain the information you submit, such as your name, contact information, profile picture, and username. Lenguista may restrict or delete Profile information if it violates the Terms of Service.
  • Video Meetings: Lenguista offers video meetings to facilitate teaching and communication between users. Inside a video meeting, you may be prompted to allow both camera and microphone access for the meeting’s purpose. All audio and video streams are encrypted through methods like Datagram Transport Layer Security (DTLS) and Secure Real-Time Protocol (SRTP). Lenguista does not record any video meetings.
  • Cookies: When you access Lenguista, we use essential session cookies to ensure the platform functions securely.
    • Authentication Cookies: Encrypted cookies used solely to keep you logged in and secure your session.
    • Security Cookies: Essential tokens used to protect your account against Cross-Site Request Forgery (CSRF) attacks and ensure the integrity of your data.
    We do not use tracking, analytics, or advertisement cookies. You can manage cookie permissions within your browser’s settings; however, cookies may be necessary to keep your account logged in and your data secure.
  • Google Services: Beyond user registration, the Service offers optional integration with Google Drive and Google Calendar to facilitate file uploads and schedule management for meetings and quizzes. These features are strictly opt-in. The Service will only access, read, or write data that you provide or that is directly relevant to course activities.

Data Retention and Deletion

All course-specific data and temporary files are permanently purged from our active systems after course completion or upon your request. We do not store your Google Drive content or Calendar history on our servers beyond what is necessary for active processing.

Personal Information Security

We never share, provide, or sell any personal information to unauthorized third parties. Any data provided is exclusively used for the platform’s intended purposes. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit using TLS 1.3. End-to-End Encryption is used whenever possible, in addition to the previously mentioned audio and video stream security protocols.
